# Read your dashboard

The **Today** tab shows how your day is going.

## Read the calorie ring

The ring shows your calories for the day.

- "**kcal left**" is what you can still eat today.
- "**kcal over**" means you ate more than your target.
- "**kcal eaten**" shows when you haven't set a target yet.

Under the ring, you see "N eaten of M kcal".

## Read the protein, carbs and fat bars

Three bars show **Protein**, **Carbs** and **Fat**, each as grams eaten against your target, for example "30 / 128 g". On a phone it reads "30" with "of 128 g" under it.

## Know what the colors mean

| Color      | Meaning                       |
| :--------- | :---------------------------- |
| Saffron    | Calories                      |
| Green      | Protein                       |
| Gold       | Carbs                         |
| Terracotta | Fat                           |
| Red        | Over your target, or an alert |

## Watch your sodium

Under the bars, you see "Sodium so far N mg of 2,000". If you go over 2,000 mg, the line turns into a warning that names the food that added most, for example "Sodium is high, mostly from" and the food.

## Read your review

With a target and something logged, you see a short review. Its title changes with the time: **Morning review**, **Afternoon review** or **Evening review**. It tells you how many calories are left or over, and which of protein, carbs or fat is furthest behind.

## See your meals

Under **Meals**, each meal has a card. It shows the meal name, the time of the first food, the calories, how much of the day it is, and its protein, carbs and fat. The biggest meal of the day has a **Biggest meal** tag.

Tap **Add to breakfast** (or the meal's name) on a card to add a food straight to that meal.

On a wide screen, you also see a card called "A note for today" with how many days you logged this week and a **See your week** link to [Trends](trends.md).
