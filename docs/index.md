# Welcome to Shumār

Shumār (شُمار) is a calorie and macro tracker made for Kashmiri food. You log what you eat, and Shumār shows your calories, protein, carbs and fat for the day against a target made for you. Every food value comes from a named source.

Your food logs are private. Only you can see them.

## Quick start

1. [Install Shumār](getting-started.md#install-shumar-on-your-phone) on your phone.
2. [Sign in and set your daily target](getting-started.md#set-your-daily-target).
3. [Log your first meal](logging-food.md#log-a-meal).
4. Check [your dashboard](your-dashboard.md) to see how the day is going.
