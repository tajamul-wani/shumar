# Get started

## Install Shumār on your phone

Shumār opens like an app and keeps working without a connection once it is on your home screen.

**On Android, in Chrome**

1. Open Shumār in Chrome.
2. Tap **Install** on the card that says "Add Shumār to your home screen".
3. Confirm. Shumār appears on your home screen.

If you chose **Not now** earlier, open the **You** tab and tap **Install Shumār** under "Install the app".

If your browser can't add Shumār as an app, the card says "Install Shumār from Chrome". Tap **Open in Chrome** or **Copy link**, then open the link in Chrome.

**On iPhone or iPad, in Safari**

1. Open Shumār in Safari.
2. Tap **Share** in Safari's toolbar.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

If you opened Shumār in another browser, the card says "Install Shumār from Safari". Tap **Copy link**, then open the link in Safari and follow the steps above.

> **Tip:** On the Today screen, tap **Show me how** on the card to see the iPhone steps.

## Sign in

1. Tap **Get started** on the first screen.
2. Answer the questions (see below).
3. On your daily target, tap **Sign in with Google to save**.

Next time, tap **Sign in** and choose the same Google account.

## Set your daily target

Shumār asks six short questions and works out how much to eat each day. Each screen shows "1 of 6", "2 of 6" and so on. Tap **Continue** after each answer. The arrow at the top takes you back.

1. **How old are you?** Enter your age in years.
2. **What is your sex?** Choose **Female** or **Male**.
3. **What do you weigh?** A rough number is fine. Choose **kg** or **lb**.
4. **How tall are you?** Enter your height in cm.
5. **How active are you on most days?** Choose **Sedentary**, **Lightly active**, **Moderately active** or **Very active**. Each choice has a short description. Count your work and your exercise together.
6. **What is your goal?** Choose **Lose weight**, **Keep my weight**, **Gain weight** or **Body recomposition**. If you choose **Lose weight**, pick **How fast?**: 0.25, 0.5 or 0.75 kg a week.

Tap **See my daily target**. You see your calories for each day, and your protein, carbs and fat targets. Tap **How this is worked out** to see the maths.

> **Tip:** You can change any answer later. Open the **You** tab and tap **Adjust target**.

If you signed in before setting a target, Today shows a card called "Set your daily target". Tap **Set my target**, answer the questions, then tap **Save my target**.
