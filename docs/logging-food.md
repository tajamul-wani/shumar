# Log food

## Log a meal

1. Tap the round **+** button above the tab bar. On a wide screen, tap **Add food** at the top right of Today.
2. Under **1. Which meal**, choose **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Evening tea**, **Dinner** or **Snack**. Shumār picks one for the time of day, and you can change it.
3. Type a food in **Search foods**, for example Girda, Haakh or eggs.
4. Tap the food you want. You see all its values.
5. Choose a unit. **Grams** is always there. Other units depend on the food, such as katori, piece or cup.
6. Set the **Quantity** with the **+** and **-** buttons, or type it. You see the calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber change as you go.
7. Tap **Add to lunch** (the button names your meal).

Shumār shows "Added to lunch" and returns you to the search box, so you can add the next food. Tap **Done** when you finish.

Under the calories and macros, you also see essential vitamins and minerals (such as calcium, iron, potassium, magnesium, zinc, vitamins and folate) and sodium for the amount you chose. Where we have no value for a food, it says **Not listed**. That is not zero.

Under **In lunch now** you see everything already in that meal and its calories.

> **Tip:** Kashmiri foods come first in the results. Tags on each result tell you more: **Kashmiri**, **Verified**, **Estimate** (a typical value, since home recipes vary) and **Yours** (a food you added).

## Log a meal of your own

If breakfast, lunch and the others don't fit, name a custom slot.

1. In the **Add food** popup, tap **Custom slot** at the end of the meal row.
2. Type a name in **Name your meal slot**, such as Chai.
3. Tap **Add slot**.

Your meal appears on Today next to the others.

## Add a food from its pack

If a food isn't in the results, add it from its label. Only you can see the foods you add.

1. Under the search box, tap **Add your own food**. If you already searched for it, its name fills in for you.
2. Enter the **Name**, and the **Brand** if the pack has one.
3. Choose **Per 100 g / ml** or **Per serving**, as the label shows. For a serving, enter what **One serving is** in grams or ml.
4. Enter **Calories**, **Protein**, **Carbs** and **Fat** from the label.
5. If the label lists them, add **Sodium**, **Calcium**, **Magnesium** and **Vitamin D**. They are optional, so leave out any the pack does not list.
6. Tap **Save food**.

Your food now shows first when you search for it, tagged **Yours**. Open it to tap **Edit** or **Remove**. Foods you already logged stay on your days.

If the numbers on the label don't add up, Shumār shows "Check these numbers". Tap **Fix the numbers**, or **Save anyway** if you are sure.

## Add yesterday's meal again

Under the search box, three tabs hold your quick adds: **Yesterday**, **Saved meals** and **My foods**. On **Yesterday** you see yesterday's matching meal, for example "Yesterday's lunch". Tap **Check yesterday's lunch** to reveal the foods eaten yesterday, or tap **Add all** to log all of it in one tap.

## Save a meal you eat often

Save a meal you eat regularly as a combo so you can log it again in one tap.

1. On the **Today** tab, go to the meal card (such as Breakfast or Lunch) where your foods are logged.
2. Tap **Save as combo**.
3. Type a name in **Name this combo**, such as Daily breakfast or Morning chai.
4. Tap **Save combo**.

Later, find it on the **Saved meals** tab in the **Add food** popup.

- To add it, tap **Add** next to its name.
- To change it, tap its name to open it. Tap **Add a food** to grow it, or remove a food to trim it.
- To delete it, open it and tap **Remove saved meal**.

## Change or remove a food

1. On Today, tap a food in its meal.
2. To change it, pick another unit, change the **Quantity** or choose another **Meal**, then tap **Save changes**.
3. To remove it, tap **Remove from breakfast** (it shows the meal's name).

After you remove a food, Shumār shows "Removed" with an **Undo** button for a few seconds. Tap **Undo** to put it back.

If the same food in the same amount appears more than once, Today shows it once with a count such as "x2". **Remove** takes away one.

## Delete an entire meal

To remove all foods in a meal at once:

1. On **Today**, go to the meal card you want to delete.
2. Tap **Delete meal**.
3. A warning dialog confirms that the entire meal and all its foods will be deleted, not just a single food item.
4. Tap **Delete entire meal** to confirm.

> **Tip:** Days you open from Trends are for reading only. To change a food, do it on Today.
