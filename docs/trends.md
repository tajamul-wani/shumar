# Read your trends

The **Trends** tab shows how your days add up.

## See your week or your month

1. Tap **Trends**.
2. Choose **Week** or **Month** at the top.
3. Use the arrows to go to an earlier or later week or month. The "later" arrow stops at the current one.

## Read the calorie bars

Each bar is one day. A line marks your target, and a bar turns red on a day you went over it. Under the bars, you see:

- **Days over target**, for example "2 of 7".
- **Days logged**.
- Your average calories a day on the days you logged.

## Read the protein, carbs and fat averages

Under **Protein, carbs and fat**, you see the average for each against its target, such as "75 / 128 g". It counts only the days you logged.

## Open a day

Tap a bar to open that day. You see its calories and macros, how close each came to your target, your sodium for the day and every meal. Tap **Trends** to go back.
