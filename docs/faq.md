# Questions and answers

## Who can see my food logs?

Only you. Your food logs are private.

## What is a katori?

A katori is a small bowl, often steel, used for serving food at home. When you pick katori as a unit, Shumār shows how many grams one katori of that food is, such as "1 katori is N g", so you know what you are logging.

## Why do I see a sodium alert?

Your sodium for the day went over 2,000 mg. The alert names the food that added the most, so you can see where it came from.

## Why does a food say Estimate?

Some foods, such as home dishes, vary from kitchen to kitchen. **Estimate** means the value is a typical one, and your own dish may differ a little.

## Can I change my daily target?

Yes. Open the **You** tab and tap **Adjust target**, or tap any row under **Your details** to change one answer. Then tap **Save my target**.

## How do I change from dark to light?

Open the **You** tab. Under **Appearance**, choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. **System** follows your phone.

## How do I get a copy of my data?

Open the **You** tab. Under **Your data**, tap **Download my data**. You get one file with your answers, your target, your food logs and the foods you added.

## How do I delete my account?

1. Open the **You** tab.
2. Under **Your data**, tap **Delete my account**.
3. Type **delete** in the box.
4. Tap **Yes, delete everything**.

Your account, your answers and all your logs go straight away. This can't be undone. Tap **Keep my account** if you change your mind. Download your data first if you want a copy.

## Where do the food values come from?

Open the **You** tab and tap **Where food values come from**. It lists every source.

## How do I sign out?

Open the **You** tab and tap **Sign out**. On a wide screen it is at the top right.
